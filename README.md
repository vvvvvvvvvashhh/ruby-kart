## 中文

在七条赛道上参加计时赛、对战赛或七站大奖赛。选择 50cc 至 250cc，搭配车手、车身、轮胎与滑翔翼。漂移积蓄加速，开伞后点按漂移键可做空中特技。游戏可在宝石红 3DS、红蓝 Switch 和珍珠白 Wii U 三款掌机界面中游玩。

离线版为 `release/MarioKart8-Ruby-3DS.html`。GitHub Pages 的发布目录为 `docs/`。默认键盘操作为 W 加速、左右方向键转向、空格漂移或特技、E 使用道具、R 复位。

## English

Race on seven circuits in Time Trial, VS Race or a seven-round Grand Prix. Choose an engine class from 50cc to 250cc and customize your driver, body, tires and glider. Charge a boost by drifting, or tap drift with the glider open to perform an aerial trick. Play on a ruby-red 3DS, a red-and-blue Switch or a pearl-white Wii U GamePad.

Open `release/MarioKart8-Ruby-3DS.html` for offline play. The GitHub Pages site is in `docs/`. Default keyboard controls are W to accelerate, the left and right arrows to steer, Space to drift or trick, E to use an item and R to recover.
