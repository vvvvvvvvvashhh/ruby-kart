# Ruby Tour

**N1n3VASthR33H**

## 游戏与实现

《Ruby Tour》把七站大奖赛装进一台可以转动的虚拟掌机。3DS 用下屏选赛道和看地图，Switch 和 Wii U 将赛事信息放在主屏。实体按键与屏幕使用同一套模型坐标，转动机身后仍可操控。

赛车以固定时间步计算转向、抓地和悬挂。四个轮胎接地点共同判断路面支撑，跳台决定起飞力度，洞顶净空决定滑翔翼能展开多少。车手在转弯和漂移时向内侧倾，双手跟随方向盘或车把。

赛道和赛车使用 WebGL 或软件光栅化绘制。固定景物预先烘焙阴影，车边路段按每格 6.25 厘米读取细节，车手、车辆和机关实时投影。原声音乐由项目内的乐谱和合成音色制作，比赛状态控制段落切换，车速与驾驶动作控制引擎和轮胎音效。

## Game and implementation

Ruby Tour fits a seven-round Grand Prix into a handheld you can rotate. The 3DS uses its lower screen for course selection and the map. The Switch and Wii U place race information on the main screen. The button hit areas and displays share the model’s coordinates, so the controls follow the hardware as it turns.

A fixed-step simulation handles steering, grip and suspension. Four tire contact points determine ground support. Ramps set the launch impulse, and overhead clearance limits how far the glider can open. Drivers lean into turns and drifts while their hands follow the wheel or handlebars.

The game draws its courses and karts with WebGL or a software rasterizer. Scenery shadows are baked offline. The road near the kart uses a 6.25 cm shadow grid, while drivers, vehicles and moving obstacles cast real-time shadows. The soundtrack uses original scores and synthesized instruments. Race events cue musical transitions, and speed and driving inputs shape the engine and tire sounds.
